Ranks one and two

The scope is D = I, diagonal N₀, and indecomposable finite T-data, modulo admissible rational time rescaling, species shifts, simultaneous relabelling and sign exchange.

Rank one: two families

Write A± = 1 + zʳ − N±. Simultaneous positivity implies N±(1) ∈ {0, 1}. The diagonal reciprocity identity implies A₊(1)A₋(1) is even: the reciprocal Laurent polynomial A₊(z)A₋(z⁻¹) has constant coefficient 2, because the two signs have disjoint support. Its nonconstant coefficients occur in equal pairs. Hence N₊(1) = N₋(1) = 1 is impossible.

If both sums vanish, N₊ = N₋ = 0 and every r > 0 is a dilation of the representative r = 1. Otherwise, exchange signs so that N₊ = 0 and N₋ = zᵖ with 0 < p < r. The symplectic identity reduces to

zᵖ + zᵖ⁻ʳ = z⁻ᵖ + zʳ⁻ᵖ.

Comparing the lowest exponents gives p − r = −p, so r = 2p. Thus the representative is (A₊, A₋) = (1 + z², 1 − z + z²). These two representatives have universal periods 2 and 5. For the second, the recurrence Y(u)Y(u − 2) = 1 + Y(u − 1) is the pentagon recurrence. Exact principal-coefficient mutation checks are saved in the records.

The first is the uncoupled A₁ recurrence. The second is the tadpole T₁ recurrence; its reconstructed connected quiver is A₂. The rank of a T-datum therefore differs from the rank of its cluster quiver.

Rank two: the six published representatives

Classes 1–6 reproduce Table 1 of Mizuno, Periodic Y-Systems and Nahm Sums: The Rank 2 Case, in that order and with the same signs. Theorem 1.5 gives completeness under change of slices; Lemmas 3.6–3.9 describe all lifts. The catalogue stores their rational rescaling and relative-shift spaces and independently recomputes their two-sided reddening certificates.

The named-family notes retain the sign and dilation conventions of Remark 1.7. In particular, a named Y-system for the opposite pair is labelled as such.

Decomposable data

Decomposable rank-two data are direct sums of two rank-one components. Each component may have its own time scale and either sign. Higher-rank direct sums are assembled from the same indecomposable entries; they are not additional indecomposable catalogue rows.
