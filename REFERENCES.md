# References and source files

- Yuma Mizuno, *Difference equations arising from cluster algebras*, doctoral
  thesis, March 2021: https://yuma-mizuno.github.io/thesis.pdf.
  The reference copy in `research/catalogue/sources/mizuno-thesis.pdf` is retained
  because its hash is part of the spectral records. Equations (3.2.1), (3.4.1),
  (3.4.2), Definition 3.4.4 and Theorem 3.4.5 are used in the spectral calculation.
- Yuma Mizuno, rank-two classification: https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.13239.
  The catalogue uses version 2, Table 1 and Theorem 1.5.
- Yuma Mizuno, T-data and periodicity: https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.05710.
- Tomoki Nakanishi and Salvatore Stella, *Wonder of sine-Gordon Y-systems*:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1212.6853.
- Anna Felikson, Michael Shapiro and Pavel Tumarkin, finite mutation-type
  classification: https://arxiv.org/abs/0811.1703.
- Sergey Fomin, Michael Shapiro and Dylan Thurston, quivers from triangulated
  surfaces: https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0608367.

`research/catalogue/sources/rsg_constructor_snapshot.py` preserves the
